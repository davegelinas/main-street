# Testimonials

Customer quotes. Short, attributed, real. **On by default.**

## How to turn it on/off

Flag: `testimonials` in `site.config.json`. Quotes live in the page markup (or a small data file if they grow past a handful).

## Rules

- **Never invent testimonials.** Every quote must come from a real customer, with their real name (first name + last initial is fine) and real permission to publish. Public Google or Yelp reviews are the exception: quote an exact excerpt, attribute "First L., Google review", and the owner's okay is enough (`rules/content.md`).
- Keep them short. One or two sentences. Trim with the customer's approval, never silently.
- Three to six quotes. Rotate them occasionally; stale praise gathers dust.
- Attribute honestly: name and, if relevant, context ("regular since 2021"). Never invent titles or credentials.

## Getting more

The best time to ask for a testimonial is right after a happy transaction. Suggest the owner ask in person and collect them in a notes file; adding them to the site is a two-minute task for their AI assistant.

## What can go wrong

- A customer asks for their quote to be removed: remove it the same day, no questions. Note the date so it doesn't get re-added from an old branch.
