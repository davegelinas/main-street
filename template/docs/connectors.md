# Plug-ins for your AI: connectors

Your AI assistant is good with words and websites. A **connector** is a plug-in that lets it also use another app, right inside your chat. Think of it like giving your assistant a new tool for its toolbox.

**Start with this: you do not need any of this.** Your AI tool plus your domain name is the whole core setup, and it gets you far. Everything below is an optional extra for later, if you ever want it. Some extras cost money; the core never does.

## When would you want one?

- **A logo or promo images** (a sale flyer, a social media graphic): connect to **Canva**.
- **A short video** for your homepage (your shop, your products, in motion): connect to **Higgsfield**.
- **Selling products online**: connect to **Shopify**.

All optional. Skip this whole page and your website is still complete.

## Canva: logos and promo images

Your AI can connect to your Canva account and design things for you without leaving the chat: a logo, a flyer, a promo image for a sale. When the design looks right, you save the finished image and your AI puts it on your site.

Two things to know:

- You need a Canva account, and the connector needs **Canva Pro or higher**. It does not work on Canva's free plan.
- Your AI will sign in to Canva with a button, the same way you'd log in. You never paste a password into the chat.

**Copy-paste:** "Can you connect to Canva? I'd like a square logo for [your business name], in [your colors, e.g. navy and gold]."

## Higgsfield: short videos

Your AI can connect to Higgsfield and generate a short video clip from a description, up to about 15 seconds. Good for a homepage hero video: your storefront at golden hour, flowers being arranged, a plumber at work.

Two things to know:

- You need a Higgsfield account. New accounts come with free credits to try it; making lots of videos costs money after that, and your AI should tell you before spending.
- The video still goes on your site through the normal loop: your AI adds it to the preview copy, you watch it on your phone, you say "ship it."

**Copy-paste:** "Connect to Higgsfield and make me a short homepage video: [describe the scene, e.g. a slow pan across a flower shop counter in the morning]."

## Shopify: selling online

If you already sell on Shopify, your AI can look up your products and feature them on your website. Every Shopify store has a built-in connection point, so there is nothing to install.

**What your site can do:**

- Show your products with photos, prices, and **Buy** buttons.
- The button takes the shopper to your Shopify checkout to pay.

**What it can't do (honest limits):**

- Your website is a simple, fast static site. The shopping cart and checkout live on **Shopify**, not inside your website. That is normal and it is how most small shops do it.
- Your AI cannot sync inventory or manage orders from your website. Those stay in your Shopify dashboard.

**Copy-paste:** "Connect to my Shopify store ([your-store].myshopify.com) and add my three best sellers to the products page with buy buttons."

Don't have Shopify yet and thinking about it? It is a paid monthly service. Ask your AI what it costs before you sign up.

## How to connect one

Just ask your AI: **"Can you connect to Canva?"** (or Higgsfield, or Shopify).

Every AI platform does this differently: a settings page, an apps list, a one-time sign-in. The exact steps change often, so instead of printed instructions that go stale, your AI will walk you through the current steps for your platform.

## If your AI says it can't

Some AI platforms don't support plug-ins yet, or they call the feature something else ("apps," "integrations," "extensions"). Try asking: **"What apps can you connect to?"**

And there is always a fallback that works everywhere: make the thing in the app yourself (design the logo in Canva, export the video from Higgsfield), then **upload the file** to your site's `content/brand` folder on GitHub ([how](gather-your-stuff.md#how-to-add-your-photos)) and tell your AI. It puts it on your site from there. The connector just saves you the trip.

## The rules still apply

- **Preview first, ship it after.** Anything a connector produces still goes on the preview copy for your approval first.
- **Never paste passwords or secret keys into chat.** Connecting uses a sign-in button, never a pasted key. Same rule as always.
- **Ask about cost before signing up.** Your AI should tell you what an app costs before you create an account.
