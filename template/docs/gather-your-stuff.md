# Gather your stuff: the weekend homework

Your website looks its best when it's built from **your** business: your photos, your words, your story. This is the one piece of homework, and you can do it in an afternoon, mostly with your phone.

Everything goes into one folder, the **shoebox**: `content/brand/`. That folder is part of your website's files, and those files are **public**, so only put in things you're happy for anyone to see. (If the words "folder" and "repo" make your eyes glaze over, skip this page and jump to ["Or just answer in chat"](#or-just-answer-in-chat) below. Same result, no files.)

## Already have a website? Start here.

If you have a current website, you can skip most of the homework below. Just give your AI the domain and it will pull your words, your photos, and your page list off the old site into the shoebox for your approval. Paste this:

> Here's my current site: [your-site.com]. Pull everything you can from it into my shoebox.

Your AI will show you what it found. You confirm what's right, fix what's outdated, and the new site gets built from that. Anything it can't reach, you fill in with the steps below.

## Photos to take with your phone

Ten minutes, daylight, phone camera. You don't need all of these; more is just better.

1. **Your storefront from the street**, phone sideways. So customers recognize you when they arrive.
2. **You at work.** Hands doing the thing. Apron on, tools out, mid-task.
3. **Your products or results, up close.** The cake, the haircut, the fixed sink, the flower arrangement.
4. **The inside.** What it feels like to walk in. Two or three angles.
5. **Your team**, if you have one. People trust people.

Put them in `content/brand/photos/` with simple names: `storefront.jpg`, `team.jpg`, `croissants.jpg`. (If your AI can reach your site files, it can do the filing if you just attach the photos in chat. A plain browser chat can't move files, so ask it for the exact GitHub clicks instead.)

**Location tip:** phone photos can carry the GPS spot where they were taken. Turn location off before you upload, because once a photo is uploaded its original stays in your site's public history: on iPhone, **Share** → **Options** → **Location** off. Your AI and an automatic check also strip it before anything reaches your live site.

What makes a good phone photo: daylight beats flash, morning and late afternoon beat harsh midday sun, and one honest photo of your real place beats ten perfect stock shots.

## Your story, in your words

Nobody knows your business like you do. Your AI needs: why you started, what you're known for, what you sell and what it costs, and what makes you different from the place down the road. There's a fill-in form waiting: `content/brand/brief.md`. It takes fifteen minutes and your AI turns your answers into the actual words on your site.

## Logo and video, if you have them

- Got a logo? Drop the file in `content/brand/logo/`. No logo? Your AI sets your business name in nice type instead. A logo is nice, never required.
- Got a short video (your shop in motion, a product being made)? `content/brand/videos/`. Phone video is fine.

## Prices and services

A simple list is perfect: "Men's cut, $35. Beard trim, $15." Put it in the brief, or paste it in chat. Prices build trust; "call for pricing" is fine when prices really do vary.

## Or just answer in chat

Don't want to touch files? You don't have to. Paste this into your AI chat:

> I want a website for my business. Interview me about it, a few questions at a time.

Your AI asks, you answer in plain words, and it fills in the brief and files everything away for you. When it asks for photos, attach them right in the chat.

## Nothing here is required

No photos yet? Half the questions unanswered? Your site still works and still looks good. The shoebox makes it *better*; it never blocks it. Add things later, one photo at a time.
